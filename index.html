ABOUT US

Our Story

Dhaura Bloom began with a simple idea: turning colourful pipe cleaners into beautiful, creative and meaningful handmade products.

We believe creativity does not always require expensive materials. With imagination, patience and attention to detail, simple materials can become something that brings happiness to another person.

Dhaura Bloom is an emerging handmade craft brand based in Thiruvottriyur, Chennai, creating attractive, affordable and customised products for people who appreciate creativity and thoughtful gifting.

What Makes Us Different?

Dhaura Bloom transforms a simple material into cute, colourful and meaningful creations. Our customers can choose from available designs or share their own ideas for colours, styles and occasions. This personal touch makes each order more than just a product—it becomes something chosen with thought.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create affordable, attractive and customised handmade products that spread happiness and encourage creativity.

Our Vision

Our vision is to grow Dhaura Bloom into a recognised handmade craft brand known for creativity, quality, personalisation and customer happiness.
